Add your signature in Gmail
  1. Fill in your details here, then click Copy signature.
  2. Open Gmail on a computer. Click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it “Jam for Cam”.
  4. Click inside the signature box and paste: Cmd + V on a Mac, Ctrl + V on Windows.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  6. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
Send yourself a test email to check it. Don’t retype or edit the signature in Gmail. To change it, make a new one here and paste it over the old one.
